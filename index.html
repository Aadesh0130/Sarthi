The problem we solve

Tourism is booming — but the gains are lopsided

A handful of hotspots overflow while hundreds of beautiful places stay unknown. Planning is fragmented and stressful, and too little of a traveller's spend ever reaches local communities. Sārthi rebalances all three.

✕
Overcrowding at a few icons — Goa, Manali, Jaipur strain under crowds while gems sit empty. → Sārthi's crowd-balancing engine nudges travellers to nearby hidden gems.
✕
Planning is fragmented & slow — hotels, transport and activities are booked across many tabs. → One AI planner builds the whole day-by-day trip and books it in a tap.
✕
Local economies are bypassed — spending pools in big chains, not with communities. → Local-first experiences route money to guides, homestays & artisans.
✕
Sustainability is an afterthought — travellers lack a simple way to see their footprint. → Every trip gets a transparent GreenTrip score with ways to improve it.
